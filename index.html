What We Do

Technical diligence

Independent assessment of scientific credibility, technical maturity, development risk, and mission architecture for investors considering space and deep-tech opportunities.

Investor intelligence

Private briefings, sector education, market mapping, and expert interpretation for investors who want to understand what is real, what is premature, and what questions have not yet been answered.

Strategic advisory

Positioning, market strategy, partnerships, and trusted introductions for select companies operating in lunar exploration, space resources, planetary science, and emerging space infrastructure.

Narrative and industry influence

Executive thought leadership, technical storytelling, and strategic communications for companies whose work is important — but difficult to explain.

Why Crisium

Space is a uniquely difficult market to evaluate.

The technology is complex. Government programs shape commercial demand. Development timelines are long. Comparisons to conventional software businesses routinely produce bad assumptions.

Crisium helps clients see beneath the pitch deck.

We connect the science, the mission architecture, the market, and the capital story — so investors can ask better questions and exceptional companies can communicate why their work matters.

Founder

Dr. Ruby Patterson

Dr. Ruby Patterson is a planetary geologist, advisor, and science communicator with experience supporting NASA lunar and Mars missions.

Her work has spanned lunar landing-site analysis, planetary remote sensing, Mars geochemistry, lunar simulants, emerging resource technologies, and the commercial development of the Moon.

She founded Crisium Group to bring scientific depth and independent judgment into the rooms where the future of the space economy is being financed and built.
